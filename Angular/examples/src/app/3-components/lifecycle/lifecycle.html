Component lifecycle

Angular runs a component through a fixed sequence of lifecycle hooks — special methods that fire at defined moments in a component's life. This child logs each one to the screen as it runs, in order, so you can watch it move through ngOnChanges, ngOnInit, and on through to ngOnDestroy.

The current subject is {{ subjectName() }}. When the parent changes it, ngOnChanges fires — the hook built for reacting to input updates.

ngDoCheck, ngAfterContentChecked, and ngAfterViewChecked fire on every change-detection pass, not just once — which is why they repeat so often in the log, and why you should avoid heavy work in them: they run constantly.
